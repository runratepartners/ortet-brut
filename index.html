What we heard

From our call on Nov 23. Correct us anywhere we're off.

  • Mobile cafe first. A custom trailer, a tight menu, events and pop-ups before any retail shelf.
  • Retail comes after. IMPASTO 01 leads, demoed in the drinks so people take it home.
  • The site's job right now is attention and signups, not orders. Think fashion magazine, not menu board.
  • Everything in one place. Shopify at the trailer and on the site, one set of numbers.
  • Spend smart. Grow off events and community, not a launch party that never pays back.
